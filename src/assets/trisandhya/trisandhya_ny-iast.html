Trikāla Sandhyā

ॐ

Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"Kwa chitukuko cha anthu, Triśandhyā kuli ngati Sañjīvanī (madzi a moyo wosafa)."

Chitani Triśandhyā pa maola onse atatu a sandhya tsiku lililonse, werengani mutu umodzi wa Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa tsiku lililonse, ndipo imbani dzina la Mādhava mosalekeza mumtima.

Gwiritsitsani ziphunzitso zotsatirazi 5 pamodzi ndi Trikāl Sandhyā

1. Phunzirani kumvera.

2. Phunzirani kudikira.

3. Phunzirani kukonda ndi kusonyeza chifundo ku zamoyo zonse.

4. Dziwani kudziletsa pa zokonda.

5. Siyani kumverera kwa “Ine ndi changa, iwe ndi chako” (kumverera kwa kudzikonda).

Malangizo Ofunika

Kuti tipulumuke ku vuto lalikulu lomwe likubwera, malinga ndi ‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā’, siyani machimo ndipo anthu onse atsatire ‘Triśandhyā’ kuti ateteze moyo. Pa nthawi yosintha kwa nthawi, kuti tilowe mu Satyayuga, mfundo yachinsinsi iyi ndi Mṛta Sañjīvanī yokhayo.
Pa nthawi yokhazikitsa Dharma, malinga ndi malangizo a Ambuye Kalki, tiyenera kutsatira zinthu izi m'miyoyo yathu:

1. Sonyezani chifundo ku zamoyo zonse. Siyani kudya nyama ndipo tsatirani moyo wa sāttvic. Musavulaze zamoyo zilizonse kapena munthu aliyense mwanjira iliyonse.

2. Mu nthawi ino, imbani dzina la Mādhava (Ambuye). Ngati mukufuna, mukhoza kuimba mokweza kapena kukumbukira dzina Lake mwachetechete mumtima.

3. Munthu aliyense ayenera kugwiritsitsa makhalidwe asanu awa: Chowonadi, Chikondi, Chifundo, Kukhululukira ndi Mtendere.

4. Munthu aliyense ayenera kutenga nawo mbali mu Satsaṅg nthawi zonse. Satsaṅg amatanthauza kusonkhana pamodzi kuimba makhalidwe a Mulungu, kufotokoza ulemerero Wake, ndi kuchita zokambirana zauzimu.

5. Chitani Triśandhyā tsiku lililonse — kumbukirani ndi kutamanda Ambuye m'mawa, masana ndi madzulo.

6. Imbani Kalki Mahāmantra yoperekedwa ndi Ambuye Kalki tsiku lililonse.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Munthu aliyense ayenera kuyenda panjira ya Dharma.

8. Kukwera pamwamba pa tsankho la mafuko ndi zipembedzo, chitani Satsaṅg ndi aliyense.

9. Siyani kumverera kwa “Ine” ndi “changa.” Kunyada ndi kumamatira kotereku kumachotsa munthu ku chikhalidwe chake chenicheni chauzimu.

10. Werengani Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa kunyumba tsiku lililonse. Nthawi ikalola, phunzirani bhajan, kīrtan, Purāṇas, malemba opatulika, Bhaviṣya Mālikā ndi mabuku ena auzimu.

11. Itanani mkazi aliyense kuti “Amayi”/“Ma” ndipo mwamuna aliyense kuti “M’bale.”

12. Musanadye, choyamba perekani chakudya kwa Ambuye.

13. Ngati mukufuna kudziwa zambiri za Ambuye Kalki ndi ntchito yayikulu iyi, funsani nambala ya foni yoperekedwa kumapeto kwa kabuku aka.


Mafunso Ofala Kwambiri

1. Mafunso wamba okhudza chizolowezi cha Triśandhyā

Triśandhyā ikhoza kuchitika popanda kusamba. Ikani dzanja lanu pamphumi panu ndipo kumbukirani Amayi Gaṅgā, ponena “Śrī Gaṅge” katatu. Ngati n'kotheka, sambani mapazi anu pasadakhale ndipo dzithireni madzi pang'ono.

Triśandhyā ikhoza kuchitika kulikonse — panjira, mu sitima, m'galimoto, pa malo anu ogwirira ntchito, kapena mwachetechete mumtima. Mwanjira ina iliyonse, pamalo ena aliwonse, iyenera kuchitika mosalephera.

Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa iyenera kuwerengedwa pokhapokha mutasamba, mukakhala oyera. Pa masiku a msambo, Triśandhyā imachitika pa nthawi zonse zitatu monga mwachizolowezi — mokweza kapena mumtima. Kwa masiku asanu ndi awiri amenewo, Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa sayenera kuwerengedwa, ngakhale ikhoza kumveka.

Pa nthawi ya sūtak nayenso, Triśandhyā ikhoza kuchitika pa nthawi zonse zitatu; koma pa nthawi imeneyo Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa sayenera kuwerengedwa kapena kumveka, ndipo Satsaṅg sayenera kulowetsedwa. Muzochitika zonsezi, Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ndi zinthu zina zopembedzera sayenera kukhudzidwa.

2. Kodi chiyenera kuchitika chiyani ngati Triśandhyā yonse siingatheke pa nthawi yoyenera?

Ngati Triśandhyā yonse siingatheke pa nthawi yoyenera, ndiye chitani Triśandhyā yochepa pa nthawi imeneyo, ndipo chitani Triśandhyā yonse pambuyo pake nthawi ikalola.

Trikāl Sandhyā ikhoza kuchitika kulikonse — pa malo ogwirira ntchito, mukuyenda panjira, mukuyendetsa galimoto, kusukulu, kapena ngakhale mukuchita ntchito ina iliyonse: mwachetechete, mumtima. Kuchita Triśandhyā pa nthawi yake, kulikonse, n'kofunika kwambiri.

Ngati Trikāl Sandhyā siingatheke pa nthawi yoyenera, nenani dzina la Ambuye Mādhava ndipo lapani, ndipo pempherani kwa Ambuye kuti kuyambira pano ichitike pa nthawi yoyenera; chifukwa malemba opatulika amati Trikāl Sandhyā iyenera kuchitika pa nthawi yake.

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate।
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Kodi ophunzira ayenera kuchita bwanji Triśandhyā pamodzi ndi zochita zawo za tsiku ndi tsiku?

Ophunzira ayenera kuchita sandhyā ya m'mawa (Brahma Muhurta (Brahma Muhūrta)) ndi madzulo pa nthawi yoyenera, malinga ndi lamulo. Chifukwa cha zofuna za sukulu kapena maphunziro, akhoza kumaliza sandhyā ya masana mumtima, akusinkhasinkha za Ambuye mumtima.

4. Kodi Triśandhyā iyenera kuchitika bwanji pa malo ogwirira ntchito?

Palibe lamulo la malo pa Triśandhyā. Mukhoza kuchita kulikonse kumene muli — pa desiki yanu, mufakitale, m'sitolo, kumunda, kapena panjira.

Ngati sizingatheke kunena mokweza, chitani mumtima; mtima ndi malo okwanira. Ngati n'kotheka, vula nsapato zanu ndipo khalani pansi kwa kanthawi; ndipo ngati ngakhale zimenezo sizingatheke, musasiye Triśandhyā chifukwa cha zimenezo.

Ngati simukukumbukira ndipo n'kotheka, mukhoza kumvera mawu a Triśandhyā. Chofunika kwambiri ndi kuti isaphonyedwe.

5. Ngati ntchito zapakhomo sizisiya nthawi, kodi chizolowezi cha Triśandhyā chiyenera kutsatiridwa bwanji?

Chitani Triśandhyā mukugwira ntchito — chifukwa cha izi palibe nthawi yopuma yofunika kudikirira; ngakhale manja ali otanganidwa ikhoza kuchitika mumtima.

Kwa Bhāgavata, tengani nthawi yochepa iliyonse yomwe mungapeze — ngati mutu wonse n'kotheka ndiye mutu, ndipo ngati sichoncho, tsamba limodzi, kapena ngakhale śloka imodzi. Kumbukirani izi, kuti palibe ngakhale tsiku limodzi limadutsa popanda kuwerenga.

6. Kodi amayi ayenera kutsatira bwanji chizolowezi cha Triśandhyā pa nthawi ya msambo?

Pa nthawi ya msambo, Bhāgavata sayenera kuwerengedwa kwa masiku asanu ndi awiri onse. Ikhoza kumveka.

Chitani Triśandhyā pa nthawi zonse zitatu. Ngati simukukumbukira, mverani.

(Musalikhudze Srimad Bhagavāta Mahapurana (Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa), kapena zinthu zina zopembedzera.)

7. Ngati munthu mmodzi m'banja atsatira chizolowezi cha Triśandhyā, kodi onse adzalandira zipatso zake?

N'kofunika kuti munthu aliyense azitsatira yekha. Ngati wina m'banja sangakwanitse kuwerenga, amvere Triśandhyā ndi Bhāgavata. Ndipo kwa iwo amene sangakwanitse kulankhula kapena kumva, yesetsani, momwe mungathere, kuti alembe dzina ‘Mādhava’ mobwerezabwereza.

8. Ngati tidya nyama ndi kumwa mowa, kodi tingayambe Triśandhyā?

Ayi, musanayambe Triśandhyā munthu ayenera kutsatira moyo wosadya nyama kwathunthu, sayenera kudya ngakhale dzira. Mtundu uliwonse wa kuledzera (monga mowa, bhang (bhāṅg), ndudu, zinthu za fodya, pān masālā, guṭkā, ndi zina zotero) uyeneranso kusiidwa motsimikiza.

9. Kodi tiyenera kusiya kudya anyezi ndi adyo kuti tiyambe Triśandhyā?

Ayi, mukhoza kuchita Triśandhyā ngakhale mutadya anyezi ndi adyo. Pokhapokha mitundu yonse ya nyama sayenera kudyedwa (kuphatikizapo dzira).

10. Kodi amayi angathe kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra ndi Triśandhyā?

Inde, amayi ayenera kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra katatu pa nthawi ya Triśandhyā.

11. Kodi tingaimbe Gāyatrī Mantra popanda Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu)?

Inde, mukhoza kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra ngakhale simuvala ulusi wopatulika wa janeu. Mukhoza kuwerenga ma mantra onse a Triśandhyā popanda kukhala dīkṣit kwa guru nayenso.

12. Kodi tingaimbe Gāyatrī Mantra popanda Śāpa Vimocana (kuchotsa temberero)?

Inde, muyenera kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra katatu pa nthawi yochita Triśandhyā, popanda kuchita kuchotsa temberero lililonse.

13. Ngati munthu ndi wosaphunzira, wosiyana maluso, mwana wakhanda kapena wokalamba, wosakhoza kuwerenga Triśandhyā, kodi ayenera kuchita chiyani?

Ngati wina sangakwanitse kuwerenga mawu a Triśandhyā, akhoza kumvera mawu ake katatu patsiku pa nthawi ya Triśandhyā. Ana akhanda amasulidwa ngati amayi awo achita Triśandhyā (adzapindula). Komabe, ayenera kuchita Triśandhyā akamakula.
