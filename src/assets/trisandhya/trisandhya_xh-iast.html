Trikāla Sandhyā

ॐ

Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"Kwi-uluntu, i-Triśandhyā kufana ne-Sañjīvanī (incindi yobuthixo)."

Yenza i-Triśandhyā ngamaxesha amathathu e-sandhyā yonke imihla, funda isahluko esinye se-Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa yonke imihla, kwaye ucule igama elithi Mādhava ngokuqhubekayo engqondweni.

Bamba ezi zifundo zi-5 zilandelayo kunye ne-Trikāl Sandhyā

1. Funda ukuthobela.

2. Funda ukulinda.

3. Funda ukuthanda nokubonisa uvelwano kuzo zonke izidalwa eziphilayo.

4. Gcina ukuzithiba phezu kweemvakalelo.

5. Lahla imvakalelo ethi “Mna neyam, wena neyakho” (ingqiqo yobuzaza).

Amaxesha Amathathu e-Triśandhyā

Kusasa kwi-Brahma Muhūrta, phambi kokuphuma kwelanga
Emini phakathi kwe-11:30 ne-12:30
Ngokuhlwa ingenziwa phakathi imizuzu engamashumi amathathu phambi nasemva kokutshona kwelanga

Imiyalelo Ebalulekileyo

Ukuze kuhlangulwe kwintlekele enkulu ezayo, ngokwe-‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā, yeka izenzo ezinesono kwaye bonke abantu mabamkele i-‘Triśandhyā ukuze kukhuselwe ubomi. Ngexesha lokuguquka kwexesha, ukuze kungene kwi-Satyayuga, lo mgaqo oyimfihlo yi-Mṛta Sañjīvanī ekuphela kwayo.
Ngexesha lokusekwa kwe-Dharma, ngokwe-miyalelo ka-Bhagavān Kalki, kufuneka silandele ezi zinto ebomini bethu:

1. Bonisa uvelwano kuzo zonke izidalwa eziphilayo. Yeka ukutya inyama kwaye wamkele ubomi be-sāttvic. Musa ukonakalisa nasiphi na isidalwa esiphilayo okanye nawuphi na umntu nangayiphi na indlela.

2. Kule minyaka, cula igama lika-Mādhava (u-Bhagavān). Ukuba unqwenela, ungacula phezulu okanye ukhumbule igama Lakhe ngokuthe cwaka engqondweni.

3. Wonke umntu kufuneka abambelele kwezi zinto zintlanu zilungileyo: iNyaniso, uThando, uvelwano, uxolelo noXolo.

4. Wonke umntu kufuneka athathe inxaxheba kwi-Satsaṅg rhoqo. I-Satsaṅg ithetha ukuhlanganisana ukuze kuculwe iimpawu zikaThixo, ukuchaza uzuko Lwakhe, nokubamba ingxoxo yasemoyeni.

5. Yenza i-Triśandhyā yonke imihla — khumbula kwaye udumise u-Bhagavān kusasa, emini nangokuhlwa.

6. Cula i-Kalki Mahāmantra enikwe ngu-Bhagavān Kalki yonke imihla.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Wonke umntu kufuneka ahambe endleleni ye-Dharma.

8. Ukuphakama ngaphezu kobuhlanga kunye nocalucalulo lwenkolo, bamba i-Satsaṅg nabo bonke abantu.

9. Lahla imvakalelo ethi “Mna” neyam.” Ukuzingca okunjalo kunye nokunamathela kuthatha umntu kude kubume bakhe bokwenyani basemoyeni.

10. Funda i-Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ekhaya yonke imihla. Njengoko ixesha livuma, funda i-bhajan, i-kīrtan, ii-Purāṇas, izibhalo, i-Bhaviṣya Mālikā kunye nezinye iitekisi zasemoyeni.

11. Biza wonke umfazi ngokuthi “Mama”/“Mā kwaye wonke indoda ngokuthi “Mzalwana.”

12. Phambi kokuba utye, qala unikele ukutya ku-Bhagavān.

13. Ukuba unqwenela ukwazi ngakumbi ngo-Bhagavān Kalki nalo msebenzi mkhulu, qhagamshelana nenombolo yeselula enikwe ekupheleni kwale ncwadana.


Imibuzo Eqhelekileyo Kakhulu

1. Imibuzo jikelele malunga nokuziqhelanisa ne-Triśandhyā

I-Triśandhyā ingenziwa ngaphandle kokuhlamba. Beka isandla sakho ebunzini lakho kwaye ukhumbule uMama u-Gaṅgā, usithi “Śrī Gaṅge kathathu. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, hlamba iinyawo zakho ngaphambili kwaye ufefe amanzi amancinci phezu kwakho.

I-Triśandhyā ingenziwa naphi na — endleleni, kwiloliwe, kwisithuthi, kwindawo yakho yomsebenzi, okanye ngokuthe cwaka entliziyweni. Ngendlela ethile, kwindawo ethile, kufuneka yenziwe ngaphandle kokusilela.

I-Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa kufuneka ifundwe kuphela emva kokuhlamba, xa icocekile. Ngexesha leentsuku zokuya esikhathini, i-Triśandhyā yenziwa ngamaxesha amathathu njengesiqhelo — phezulu okanye entliziyweni. Kwezo ntsuku zisixhenxe, i-Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ayifundwa, nangona ingenziwa ukuba imanyelwe.

Ngexesha le-sūtak nalo, i-Triśandhyā ingenziwa ngamaxesha amathathu; kodwa ngelo xesha i-Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ayifundwa kwaye ayimanyelwa, kwaye i-Satsaṅg akufuneki ijoyinwe. Kuzo zombini ezi meko, i-Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa kunye nezinye izinto zonqulo akufuneki zichukunyiswe.

2. Kufuneka kwenziwe ntoni ukuba i-Triśandhyā epheleleyo ayinakwenzeka ngexesha elifanelekileyo?

Ukuba i-Triśandhyā epheleleyo ayinakwenzeka ngexesha elifanelekileyo, yenza i-Triśandhyā emfutshane ngelo xesha, kwaye yenza i-Triśandhyā epheleleyo kamva xa ixesha livuma.

I-Trikāl Sandhyā ingenziwa naphi na — kwindawo yomsebenzi, ngelixa uhamba endleleni, ngelixa uqhuba, esikolweni, okanye nangona usenza nawuphi na omnye umsebenzi: ngokuthe cwaka, engqondweni. Ukwenza i-Triśandhyā ngexesha layo elifanelekileyo, naphi na, kubaluleke kakhulu.

Ukuba i-Trikāl Sandhyā ayinakwenzeka ngexesha elifanelekileyo, thabatha igama lika-Bhagavān Mādhava kwaye uguquke, kwaye uthandaze ku-Bhagavān ukuba ukususela ngoku ingenziwa ngexesha elifanelekileyo; kuba izibhalo zibhengeza ukuba i-Trikāl Sandhyā kufuneka yenziwe ngexesha layo elifanelekileyo.

Indlela ye-Trikāl Sandhyā xa kungekho xesha

I-śloka enikwe ngu-Paṇḍitjī kwi-Google Meet. Xa ithuba lokwenza i-sandhyā lingafiki kwaye ixesha liqala ukudlula, ngoko endaweni ye-sandhyā ungacula igama lika-Mādhava 108 amaxesha, Jai Durgā Mādhava 11 amaxesha, kwaye ucengceleze i-śloka enikwe ngezantsi kanye.

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Abafundi kufuneka benze njani i-Triśandhyā kunye nenkqubo yabo yemihla ngemihla?

Abafundi kufuneka benze i-sandhyā yasekuseni (Brahma Muhūrta) neyangokuhlwa ngexesha elifanelekileyo, ngokomthetho. Ngenxa yeemfuno zesikolo okanye zokufunda, bangayigqiba i-sandhyā yasemini entliziyweni, becamngca ngo-Bhagavān engqondweni.

4. Kufuneka yenziwe njani i-Triśandhyā kwindawo yomsebenzi?

Akukho mthetho wendawo kwi-Triśandhyā. Ungayenza naphi na apho ukhoyo — edesikeni yakho, efektri, evenkileni, entsimini, okanye endleleni.

Ukuba akunakwenzeka ukulitsho phezulu, yenza entliziyweni; intliziyo yindawo eyaneleyo. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, khulula izihlangu zakho kwaye uhlale okomzuzwana; kwaye ukuba nangona oko kungenakwenzeka, musa ukuyishiya i-Triśandhyā ngenxa yoko.

Ukuba awuyikhumbuli kwaye kunokwenzeka, unokumamele iaudio ye-Triśandhyā. Eyona nto ibalulekileyo kukuba ingaphoswa.

5. Ukuba umsebenzi wasekhaya awushiyi xesha, kufuneka kulandelwe njani ukuziqhelanisa ne-Triśandhyā?

Yenza i-Triśandhyā ngelixa usebenza — kuba oku akukho xesha lasimahla kufuneka lilindelwe; nangona izandla zixakekile ingenziwa entliziyweni.

Kwi-Bhāgavata, thabatha naliphi na ixesha elincinci onokulifumana — ukuba isahluko esipheleleyo sinokwenzeka ngoko isahluko, kwaye ukuba akunjalo, iphepha elinye, okanye nangona i-śloka enye. Makukhunjulwe oku, ukuba akukho nanye imini edlulayo ngaphandle kokufunda.

6. Abafazi kufuneka balandele njani ukuziqhelanisa ne-Triśandhyā ngexesha lokuya esikhathini?

Ngexesha lokuya esikhathini, i-Bhāgavata ayifundwa iintsuku ezisixhenxe ezipheleleyo. Ingenziwa ukuba imanyelwe.

Yenza i-Triśandhyā ngamaxesha amathathu. Ukuba awuyikhumbuli, yimamele.

(Musa ukuchukumisa i-Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, okanye ezinye izinto zonqulo.)

7. Ukuba umntu omnye kusapho ulandela ukuziqhelanisa ne-Triśandhyā, ngaba bonke baya kufumana isiqhamo sayo?

Kubalulekile ukuba wonke umntu ayilandele ngokwakhe. Ukuba umntu kusapho akakwazi ukufunda, mabamamele i-Triśandhyā ne-Bhāgavata. Kwaye kwabo bangakwaziyo ukuthetha okanye ukuva, yenza umzamo, kangangoko kunokwenzeka, ukuba bababhale igama elithi ‘Mādhava ngokuphindaphindiweyo.

8. Ukuba sitya inyama kwaye sisela utywala, singaqala i-Triśandhyā?

Hayi, phambi kokuqala i-Triśandhyā umntu kufuneka amkele indlela yokuphila yemifuno ngokupheleleyo, akufuneki atyi nangona iqanda. Naluphi na uhlobo lokunxila (njengotywala, i-bhāṅg, icuba, iimveliso zecuba, i-pān masālā, i-guṭkā, njl.) kufuneka nalo luyekwe ngokuqinisekileyo.

9. Ngaba kufuneka sizile i-anyanisi negalikhi ukuze siqale i-Triśandhyā?

Hayi, ungenza i-Triśandhyā nangona utya i-anyanisi negalikhi. Kuphela zonke iintlobo zenyama akufuneki zityiwe (kubandakanya iqanda).

10. Ngaba abafazi bangacula i-Gāyatrī Mantra ne-Triśandhyā?

Ewe, abafazi kufuneka bacule i-Gāyatrī Mantra kathathu ngexesha le-Triśandhyā.

11. Ngaba singacula i-Gāyatrī Mantra ngaphandle kwe-Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu)?

Ewe, ungacula i-Gāyatrī Mantra nangona unganxibi umsonto ongcwele we-janeu (janeu). Ungacengceleza zonke ii-mantras ze-Triśandhyā ngaphandle kokuba u-dīkṣit guru nalo.

12. Ngaba singacula i-Gāyatrī Mantra ngaphandle kwe-Śāpa Vimocana (ukuxolelwa kwesiqalekiso)?

Ewe, kufuneka ucule i-Gāyatrī Mantra kathathu ngexesha lokwenza i-Triśandhyā, ngaphandle kokwenza naluphi na uxolelwa kwesiqalekiso.

13. Ukuba umntu akafundanga, unesiphene, usana okanye umntu omdala, ongenakukwazi ukufunda i-Triśandhyā, kufuneka enze ntoni?

Ukuba umntu akakwazi ukufunda umbhalo we-Triśandhyā, bangakwazi ukumamele iaudio yayo kathathu ngosuku ngexesha le-Triśandhyā. Iintsana zikhululwe ukuba oomama bazo benza i-Triśandhyā (ziya kuxhamla). Nangona kunjalo, kufuneka ziziqhelanise ne-Triśandhyā njengoko zikhula.
