Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"Kune budiriro yevanhu, Triśandhyā yakafanana ne Sañjīvanī (mvura youmwari)."

Ita Triśandhyā panguva dzose nhatu dze sandhyā zuva rega rega, verenga chitsauko chimwe che Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa zuva ne zuva, uye imba zita ra Mādhava nguva dzose mupfungwa.

Bata dzidzo shanu dzinotevera pamwe chete ne Trikāl Sandhyā

1. Dzidza kuteerera.

2. Dzidza kumirira.

3. Dzidza kuda uye kuratidza tsitsi kuzvisikwa zvose zvipenyu.

4. Chengetedza kuzvidzora pamusoro pekunzwa.

5. Siya pfungwa ye “Ini neyangu, iwe neyako” (pfungwa ye ego).

Mirayiridzo Yakakosha

Kuti tiponeswe kubva padambudziko guru riri kuuya, maererano ne ‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā, siyana nemabasa ezvivi uye regai vanhu vose vatore ‘Triśandhyā kuchengetedza hupenyu. Panguva yekushanduka kwezera, yekupinda mu Satyayuga, iyi nheyo yakavanzika ndiyo yega Mṛta Sañjīvanī.
Panguva yekugadzwa kwe Dharma, maererano ne mirayiridzo ya Bhagavān Kalki, tinofanira kutevera zvinhu izvi muhupenyu hwedu:

1. Ratidza tsitsi kuzvisikwa zvose zvipenyu. Siya kudya nyama uye tora hupenyu hwe sāttvic. Usakuvadza chero chisikwa chipenyu kana chero munhu nenzira ipi zvayo.

2. Muzera rino, imba zita ra Mādhava (Bhagavān). Kana uchida, unogona kuimba zvinonzwika kana kuyeuka Zita Rake chinyararire mupfungwa.

3. Munhu wese anofanira kubata kune hunhu uhu hushanu: Chokwadi, Rudo, Tsitsi, Kuregerera uye Runyararo.

4. Munhu wese anofanira kutora chikamu mu Satsaṅg nguva dzose. Satsaṅg zvinoreva kuungana pamwe chete kuimba hunhu hwa Mwari, kutsanangura kubwinya Kwake, uye kuita hurukuro yemweya.

5. Ita Triśandhyā zuva rega rega — yeuka uye rumbidza Bhagavān mangwanani, masikati uye manheru.

6. Imba Kalki Mahāmantra yakapiwa na Bhagavān Kalki zuva rega rega.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Munhu wese anofanira kufamba panzira ye Dharma.

8. Kukwira pamusoro perusarura rwerudzi nerwechitendero, ita Satsaṅg nemunhu wese.

9. Siya pfungwa ye “Ini” ne “yangu.” Kuzvikudza kwakadaro uye kubatana zvinotora munhu kure nehunhu hwake hwechokwadi hwemweya.

10. Verenga Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa kumba zuva rega rega. Sezvo nguva ichibvumira, dzidza bhajan, kīrtan, Purāṇas, magwaro, Bhaviṣya Mālikā nemamwe magwaro emweya.

11. Taura nemukadzi wese se “Amai”/“Mā uye murume wese se “Hama.”

12. Usati wadya, tanga wapa chikafu kuna Bhagavān.

13. Kana uchida kuziva zvakawanda nezva Bhagavān Kalki nebasa guru iri, bata nhamba yefoni yakapiwa kumagumo kebhurashura iri.


Mibvunzo Inonyanya Kubvunzwa

1. Mibvunzo yakajairika nezve tsika ye Triśandhyā

Triśandhyā inogona kuitwa pasina kugeza. Isa ruoko rwako pahuma yako uye yeuka Amai Gaṅgā, uchiti “Śrī Gaṅge katatu. Kana zvichibvira, geza tsoka dzako usati waita uye uzvinyunyutire mvura shoma.

Triśandhyā inogona kuitwa chero kupi — munzira, muchitima, mumotokari, panzvimbo yako yebasa, kana chinyararire mumoyo. Mune imwe nzira kana imwe, pane imwe nzvimbo kana imwe, inofanira kuitwa pasina kukundikana.

Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa inofanira kuverengwa chete mushure mekugeza, kana yakachena. Pamazuva ekuenda kumwedzi Triśandhyā inoitwa panguva dzose nhatu sezvakajairwa — zvinonzwika kana mumoyo. Kwemazuva manomwe iwayo Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa haifaniri kuverengwa, kunyange zvazvo ichigona kunzwika.

Panguva ye sūtak zvakare, Triśandhyā inogona kuitwa panguva dzose nhatu; asi panguva iyoyo Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa haifaniri kuverengwa kana kunzwika, uye Satsaṅg haifaniri kupindwa. Muzviitiko zvose zviri zviviri Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa nezvimwe zvinhu zvekunamata hazvifaniri kubatwa.

2. Chii chinofanira kuitwa kana Triśandhyā yakazara isingakwanisiki panguva yakakodzera?

Kana Triśandhyā yakazara isingakwanisiki panguva yakakodzera, ipapo ita pfupi Triśandhyā panguva iyoyo, uye ita yakazara Triśandhyā gare gare kana nguva ichibvumira.

Trikāl Sandhyā inogona kuitwa chero kupi — panzvimbo yebasa, uchifamba munzira, uchityaira, kuchikoro, kana kunyange uchiita chero rimwe basa: chinyararire, mupfungwa. Kuita Triśandhyā panguva yayo yakakodzera, chero panzvimbo ipi zvayo, kwakakosha zvakanyanya.

Kana Trikāl Sandhyā isingakwanisiki kuitwa panguva yakakodzera, tora zita ra Bhagavān Mādhava uye pfidza, uye nyengetera kuna Bhagavān kuti kubva zvino zvichienda mberi inogona kuitwa panguva yakakodzera; nekuti magwaro anozivisa kuti Trikāl Sandhyā inofanira kuitwa panguva yayo yakakodzera.

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate।
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Vadzidzi vanofanira kuita sei Triśandhyā pamwe chete nemaitiro avo ezuva nezuva?

Vadzidzi vanofanira kuita sandhyā yemangwanani (Brahma Muhūrta) neyemanheru panguva yakakodzera, maererano nemutemo. Nekuda kwezvinodiwa zvechikoro kana kudzidza, vanogona kupedzisa sandhyā yemasikati mumoyo, vachifungisisa nezva Bhagavān mupfungwa.

4. Triśandhyā inofanira kuitwa sei panzvimbo yebasa?

Hapana mutemo wenzvimbo ye Triśandhyā. Unogona kuiita chero kwauri — padhesiki rako, mufekitori, muchitoro, mumunda, kana munzira.

Kana zvisingakwanisiki kutaura zvinonzwika, ita mumoyo; moyo inzvimbo yakakwana. Kana zvichibvira, bvisa shangu dzako ugare pasi kwechinguva; uye kana kunyange izvozvo zvisingakwanisiki, usasiya Triśandhyā nekuda kwechikonzero ichocho.

Kana usingayeuki uye zvichibvira, unogona kuteerera odhiyo ye Triśandhyā. Chinhu chikuru ndechekuti isapotsa.

5. Kana basa repamba risingasiyi nguva, tsika ye Triśandhyā inofanira kuteverwa sei?

Ita Triśandhyā uchishanda — nekuda kweizvi hapana nguva yekusununguka inofanira kumirirwa; kunyange maoko achibatikana inogona kuitwa mumoyo.

Kune Bhāgavata, tora chero nguva shoma yaunogona kuwana — kana chitsauko chakazara chichibvira ipapo chitsauko, uye kana zvisiri, peji rimwe chete, kana kunyange śloka imwe chete. Izvi ngazvirangarirwe, kuti kunyange zuva rimwe chete haripfuuri pasina kuverenga.

6. Vakadzi vanofanira kutevera sei tsika ye Triśandhyā panguva yekuenda kumwedzi?

Panguva yekuenda kumwedzi, Bhāgavata haifaniri kuverengwa kwemazuva manomwe akazara. Inogona kunzwika.

Ita Triśandhyā panguva dzose nhatu. Kana usingayeuki, teerera.

(Usabata Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, kana zvimwe zvinhu zvekunamata.)

7. Kana munhu mumwe chete mumhuri achitevera tsika ye Triśandhyā, vose vachagamuchira muchero wayo here?

Zvakakosha kuti munhu wese azvitevere pachake. Kana mumwe munhu mumhuri asingagoni kuverenga, ngavanzwe Triśandhyā ne Bhāgavata. Uye kune avo vasingagoni kutaura kana kunzwa, ita nhamburiko, sezvinobvira, kuti vanyore zita ra ‘Mādhava kakawanda.

8. Kana tichidya nyama uye tichinwa doro, tinogona kutanga Triśandhyā here?

Kwete, usati watanga Triśandhyā munhu anofanira kutora mararamiro ezvinomera zvakakwana, haafaniri kunyange kudya zai. Chero rudzi rwekudhakwa (senge doro, bhāṅg, fodya, zvigadzirwa zvefodya, pān masālā, guṭkā, nezvimwe) zvinofanirawo kumiswa zvakasimba.

9. Tinofanira kurega hanyanisi negariki kuti titange Triśandhyā here?

Kwete, unogona kuita Triśandhyā kunyange kana uchidya hanyanisi negariki. Chete marudzi ose enyama haafaniri kudyiwa (kusanganisira zai).

10. Vakadzi vanogona kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra ne Triśandhyā here?

Hongu, vakadzi vanofanira kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra katatu panguva ye Triśandhyā.

11. Tinogona kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra pasina Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu) here?

Hongu, unogona kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra kunyange kana usingapfeki tambo tsvene ye janeu (janeu). Unogona kudzokorora mantras ose e Triśandhyā pasina kuva dīkṣit kuna guru zvakare.

12. Tinogona kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra pasina Śāpa Vimocana (kuregererwa kwekutuka) here?

Hongu, unofanira kuimba Gāyatrī Mantra katatu panguva yekuita Triśandhyā, pasina kuita chero kuregererwa kwekutuka.

13. Kana munhu asingagoni kuverenga, akaremara, mucheche kana mukuru, asingagoni kuverenga Triśandhyā, vanofanira kuita sei?

Kana mumwe munhu asingagoni kuverenga rugwaro rwe Triśandhyā, anogona kuteerera odhiyo yayo katatu pazuva panguva ye Triśandhyā. Vacheche vanoregererwa kana vanaamai vavo vachiita Triśandhyā (vachabatsirwa). Zvisinei, vanofanira kuita Triśandhyā pavanokura.
