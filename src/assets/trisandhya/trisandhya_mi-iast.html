Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"Mō te ao tangata, ko Triśandhyā he rite ki te Sañjīvanī (wai ora atua)."

Mahia te Triśandhyā i ngā wā sandhyā e toru ia rā, pānuihia tētahi upoko o te Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ia rā, me te waiata i te ingoa Mādhava i roto i te hinengaro i ngā wā katoa.

Puritia ngā akoranga e 5 e whai ake nei me te Trikāl Sandhyā

1. Akohia te whakarongo.

2. Akohia te tatari.

3. Akohia te aroha me te atawhai ki ngā mea ora katoa.

4. Kia mau te here ki ngā pūmanawa.

5. Tukua te kare-ā-roto o “Ko au me aku, ko koe me ōu” (te kare-ā-roto o te ego).

Ngā Tohutohu Nui

Mō te whakaoranga mai i te raru nui e haere mai nei, e ai ki te ‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā’, whakarerea ngā mahi hara, ā, kia tangohia e ngā tāngata katoa te ‘Triśandhyā’ mō te tiaki i te ora. I te wā o te hurihanga o te tau, mō te urunga ki Satyayuga, ko tēnei mātāpono ngaro te Mṛta Sañjīvanī kotahi anake.
I te wā o te whakapūmautanga o Dharma, e ai ki ngā tohutohu a Te Ariki Kalki, me whai tātou i ēnei mea i roto i ō tātou oranga:

1. Whakaatuhia te atawhai ki ngā mea ora katoa. Tukua te kai mīti me te tango i te oranga sāttvic. Kaua e tukino i tētahi mea ora, i tētahi tangata rānei i tētahi ara.

2. I tēnei tau, waiatahia te ingoa o Mādhava (Te Ariki). Ki te hiahia koe, ka taea e koe te waiata nui, te mahara rānei ki Tōna ingoa ā-puku i roto i te hinengaro.

3. Me mau ia tangata ki ēnei tikanga e rima: Pono, Aroha, Atawhai, Murunga hara me te Rangimārie.

4. Me whai wāhi ia tangata ki te Satsaṅg i ngā wā katoa. Ko te Satsaṅg te tikanga o te huihui tahi ki te waiata i ngā āhuatanga o te Atua, ki te whakaahua i Tōna korōria, me te whakahaere i ngā kōrero wairua.

5. Mahia te Triśandhyā ia rā — mahara me te whakanui i Te Ariki i te ata, i te aotearoa me te ahiahi.

6. Waiatahia te Kalki Mahāmantra i homai e Te Ariki Kalki ia rā.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Me haere ia tangata i runga i te ara o Dharma.

8. Ka piki ake i runga i te wehewehenga iwi me te whakapono, puritia te Satsaṅg me te katoa.

9. Tukua te kare-ā-roto o “Ko au” me “aku.” Ko taua whakapehapeha me te piri ka kawe i te tangata ki tawhiti atu i tōna āhua wairua tūturu.

10. Pānuihia te Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa i te kāinga ia rā. I te wā e wātea ana, akohia te bhajan, kīrtan, ngā Purāṇas, ngā karaipiture, te Bhaviṣya Mālikā me ētahi atu tuhinga wairua.

11. Karangatia ia wahine ko “Whaea”/“Mā” me ia tāne ko “Tuakana.”

12. I mua i te kai, tuatahi tukua te kai ki Te Ariki.

13. Ki te hiahia koe ki te mōhio atu mō Te Ariki Kalki me tēnei mahi nui, whakapā atu ki te nama waea pūkoro i hoatu i te mutunga o tēnei pukapuka iti.


Ngā Pātai Nui rawa atu

1. Ngā pātai whānui mō te mahi Triśandhyā

Ka taea te mahi Triśandhyā kāore he kaukau. Whakanohoia tō ringa ki tō rae ka mahara ki Whaea Gaṅgā, me te kī “Śrī Gaṅge” kia toru ngā wā. Ki te taea, horoia ō waewae i mua ka tauhiuhia he wai iti ki runga i a koe.

Ka taea te mahi Triśandhyā ki hea — i te rori, i roto i te tereina, i roto i te waka, i tō wāhi mahi, rānei ā-puku i roto i te ngākau. I tētahi āhua, i tētahi wāhi rānei, me mahi tonu me te kore e ngaro.

Me pānui te Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa i muri anake i te kaukau, i te wā e mā ana. I ngā rā o te paheketanga ka mahia te Triśandhyā i ngā wā e toru i ngā wā katoa — nui, ā-puku rānei. Mō aua rā e whitu kāore e pānuihia te Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, engari ka taea te whakarongo.

I te wā o sūtak hoki, ka taea te mahi Triśandhyā i ngā wā e toru; engari i taua wā kāore e pānuihia, e whakarongohia rānei te Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, ā, kaua e uru ki te Satsaṅg. I ngā take e rua kāore e pa te Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa me ētahi atu taonga karakia.

2. He aha te mahi mena kāore e taea te Triśandhyā katoa i te wā tika?

Mena kāore e taea te Triśandhyā katoa i te wā tika, mahia te Triśandhyā poto i taua wā, ka mahi i te Triśandhyā katoa ā muri ake ka wātea te wā.

Ka taea te mahi Trikāl Sandhyā ki hea — i te wāhi mahi, i te wā e hīkoi ana i te rori, i te wā e taraiwa ana, i te kura, tae noa ki te wā e mahi ana i ētahi atu mahi: ā-puku, i roto i te hinengaro. Ko te mahi i te Triśandhyā i tōna wā tika, ahakoa ki hea, he mea tino tika.

Mena kāore e taea te mahi Trikāl Sandhyā i te wā tika, tangohia te ingoa o Te Ariki Mādhava ka whakapāha, ka inoi ki Te Ariki kia taea ai te mahi i te wā tika mai i tēnei wā; nō te mea e kī ana ngā karaipiture me mahi te Trikāl Sandhyā i tōna wā tika.

Te tikanga o te Trikāl Sandhyā i te wā kāore he wā

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate।
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Me pēhea ngā ākonga e mahi Triśandhyā me ō rātou mahi o ia rā?

Me mahi ngā ākonga i te sandhyā o te ata (Brahma Muhūrta) me te ahiahi i te wā tika, e ai ki te ture. Nā te hiahia o te kura, o te ako rānei, ka taea e rātou te whakaoti i te sandhyā o te aotearoa i roto i te ngākau, me te whakaaroaro ki Te Ariki i roto i te hinengaro.

4. Me pēhea te mahi Triśandhyā i te wāhi mahi?

Kāore he ture wāhi mō Triśandhyā. Ka taea e koe te mahi ki hea koe — i tō tēpu, i roto i te wheketere, i roto i te toa, i roto i te mara, rānei i te rori.

Mena kāore e taea te kī nui, mahia i roto i te ngākau; he nui noa te ngākau. Ki te taea, tangohia ō hu ka noho mō te wā poto; ā, mena kāore hoki tērā e taea, kaua e whakarere i te Triśandhyā mō tērā take.

Mena kāore koe e mahara, ā, ki te taea, ka taea e koe te whakarongo ki te ororongo Triśandhyā. Ko te mea nui kia kaua e ngaro.

5. Mena kāore he wā i ngā mahi o te kāinga, me pēhea te whai i te mahi Triśandhyā?

Mahia te Triśandhyā i te wā e mahi ana koe — mō tēnei kāore e tatarihia he wā wātea; ahakoa e pukumahi ana ngā ringa ka taea te mahi i roto i te ngākau.

Mō te Bhāgavata, tangohia te wā iti e kitea e koe — mena ka taea he upoko katoa, he upoko, ā, mena kāore, he whārangi kotahi, tae noa ki te śloka kotahi. Kia maharatia tēnei, kia kaua e pahemo tētahi rā me te kore e pānui.

6. Me pēhea ngā wāhine e whai i te mahi Triśandhyā i te wā o te paheketanga?

I te wā o te paheketanga, kāore e pānuihia te Bhāgavata mō ngā rā e whitu katoa. Ka taea te whakarongo.

Mahia te Triśandhyā i ngā wā e toru. Mena kāore koe e mahara, whakarongo ki a ia.

(Kaua e pa ki te Hrīmad Bāgavata Mahāpurāna (Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa), me ētahi atu taonga karakia.)

7. Mena ka whai tētahi tangata i roto i te whānau i te mahi Triśandhyā, ka riro i a rātou katoa ōna hua?

He mea tika kia whai ia tangata i a ia anō. Mena kāore e taea e tētahi i roto i te whānau te pānui, me whakarongo rātou ki te Triśandhyā me te Bhāgavata. Ā, mō te hunga kāore e taea te kōrero, te whakarongo rānei, whakapau kaha, i te mea e taea ana, kia tuhia e rātou te ingoa ‘Mādhava’ anō, anō.

8. Mena ka kai mīti kore-huawhenua mātou ka inu waipiro, ka taea e mātou te timata Triśandhyā?

Kao, i mua i te timatanga o Triśandhyā me tango tētahi āhua noho kai huawhenua katoa, kaua e kai hoki i te hēki. Me whakamutu hoki ngā momo haurangi katoa (pērā i te waipiro, bāng (bhāṅg), hikareti, hua tupeka, pān masālā, gutkā, aha atu) mā te tino.

9. Me karo mātou i te Aniana me te Kāriki kia timata Triśandhyā?

Kao, ka taea e koe te mahi Triśandhyā ahakoa ka kai koe i te Aniana me te kāriki. Ko ngā momo kai kore-huawhenua katoa anake me karo (tae atu ki te hēki).

10. Ka taea e ngā wāhine te waiata Gāyatrī Mantra me Triśandhyā?

Āe, me waiata ngā wāhine i te Gāyatrī Mantra kia toru ngā wā i te wā o Triśandhyā.

11. Ka taea e mātou te waiata Gāyatrī Mantra me te kore Te Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu)?

Āe, ka taea e koe te waiata Gāyatrī Mantra ahakoa kāore koe e mau i te miro tapu janeu. Ka taea e koe te whakahua i ngā mantra katoa o Triśandhyā me te kore e dīkṣit ki tētahi guru hoki.

12. Ka taea e mātou te waiata Gāyatrī Mantra me te kore Śāpa Vimocana (whakaoranga kanga)?

Āe, me waiata koe i te Gāyatrī Mantra kia toru ngā wā i te wā e mahi ana i te Triśandhyā, me te kore e mahi i tētahi whakaoranga kanga.

13. Mena he kore-pānui tētahi, he haua, he pēpi, he kaumātua rānei, kāore e kaha ki te pānui Triśandhyā, me aha rātou?

Mena kāore e kaha tētahi ki te pānui i te tuhinga Triśandhyā, ka taea e rātou te whakarongo ki tōna ororongo kia toru ngā wā i te rā i te wā o Triśandhyā. Ka whakawāteatia ngā pēpi mena ka mahi ō rātou whaea i te Triśandhyā (ka whai hua rātou). Heoi, me mahi rātou i te Triśandhyā i a rātou e tipu ake ana.
