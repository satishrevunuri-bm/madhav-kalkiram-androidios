Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"No ka nohona kanaka, ua like ʻo Triśandhyā me Sañjīvanī (ka wai ola hemolele)."

E hana i ka Triśandhyā i nā hola sandhyā ʻekolu i kēlā me kēia lā, e heluhelu i hoʻokahi mokuna o ka Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa i kēlā me kēia lā, a e oli mau i ka inoa Mādhava i loko o ka manaʻo.

E paʻa i kēia mau aʻo 5 me ka Trikāl Sandhyā

1. E aʻo e hoʻolohe.

2. E aʻo e kali.

3. E aʻo e aloha a e hōʻike i ke aloha i nā mea ola a pau.

4. E mālama i ka hoʻopaʻa ʻana ma luna o nā manaʻo.

5. E haʻalele i ka manaʻo o “ʻO wau a me kaʻu, ʻo ʻoe a me kāu” (ka manaʻo haʻaheo).

Jaya Śrī Mādhava


Nā ʻŌlelo Aʻo Nui

No ka hoʻopakele ʻana mai ka pilikia nui e hiki mai ana, e like me ka ‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā’, e haʻalele i nā hana hewa a e ʻae nā kānaka a pau i ka ‘Triśandhyā’ no ka pale ʻana i ke ola. I ka manawa o ka hoʻololi ʻana o ka makahiki, no ke komo ʻana i Satyayuga, ʻo kēia kumu huna ka Mṛta Sañjīvanī hoʻokahi wale nō.
I ka manawa o ka hoʻokumu ʻana o Dharma, e like me nā ʻōlelo aʻo a ka Haku Kalki, pono mākou e hahai i kēia mau mea i loko o ko mākou ola:

1. E hōʻike i ke aloha i nā mea ola a pau. E haʻalele i ka ʻai ʻana i ka ʻiʻo a e ʻae i kahi ola sāttvic. Mai hōʻino i kekahi mea ola a i ʻole kekahi kanaka ma kekahi ʻano.

2. I kēia au, e oli i ka inoa o Mādhava (ka Haku). Inā makemake ʻoe, hiki iā ʻoe ke oli leo nui a i ʻole e hoʻomanaʻo i kona inoa me ka leo ʻole i loko o ka manaʻo.

3. Pono kēlā me kēia kanaka e paʻa i kēia mau pono ʻelima: ʻoiaʻiʻo, aloha, aloha, kala ʻana a me ka maluhia.

4. Pono kēlā me kēia kanaka e komo mau i ka Satsaṅg. ʻO Satsaṅg ke ʻano o ka hōʻuluʻulu ʻana e hīmeni i nā ʻano o ke Akua, e wehewehe i kona nani, a e kūkākūkā i nā mea ʻuhane.

5. E hana i ka Triśandhyā i kēlā me kēia lā — e hoʻomanaʻo a e hoʻomaikaʻi i ka Haku i ke kakahiaka, awakea a me ke ahiahi.

6. E oli i ka Kalki Mahāmantra i hāʻawi ʻia e ka Haku Kalki i kēlā me kēia lā.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Pono kēlā me kēia kanaka e hele ma ke ala o Dharma.

8. Ke piʻi aʻe ma luna o ka hoʻokae lāhui a me ka hoʻomana, e paʻa i ka Satsaṅg me nā mea a pau.

9. E haʻalele i ka manaʻo o “ʻO wau” a me “kaʻu.” ʻO ia ʻano haʻaheo a me ka pili e lawe aku i ke kanaka mai kona ʻano ʻuhane maoli.

10. E heluhelu i ka Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ma ka home i kēlā me kēia lā. Ke ʻae ka manawa, e aʻo i ka bhajan, kīrtan, nā Purāṇas, nā palapala hemolele, ka Bhaviṣya Mālikā a me nā kikokikona ʻuhane ʻē aʻe.

11. E kapa i kēlā me kēia wahine he “Māmā”/“Mā” a i kēlā me kēia kāne he “Kaikunāne.”

12. Ma mua o ka ʻai ʻana, e hāʻawi mua i ka meaʻai i ka Haku.

13. Inā makemake ʻoe e ʻike hou aku e pili ana i ka Haku Kalki a me kēia hana nui, e hoʻokaʻaʻike i ka helu kelepona i hāʻawi ʻia ma ka hope o kēia puke liʻiliʻi.


Nā Nīnau Maʻamau Loa

1. Nā nīnau maʻamau e pili ana i ka hana Triśandhyā

Hiki ke hana ʻia ka Triśandhyā me ka ʻauʻau ʻole. E kau i kou lima ma kou lae a e hoʻomanaʻo i ka Māmā Gaṅgā, me ka ʻōlelo ʻana “Śrī Gaṅge” ʻekolu manawa. Inā hiki, e holoi mua i kou mau wāwae a e kāpīpī i kahi wai liʻiliʻi ma luna ou.

Hiki ke hana ʻia ka Triśandhyā ma nā wahi a pau — ma ke alanui, i loko o ke kaʻaahi, i loko o ke kaʻa, ma kou wahi hana, a i ʻole me ka leo ʻole i loko o ka puʻuwai. Ma kekahi ʻano a i ʻole kekahi, ma kekahi wahi a i ʻole kekahi, pono e hana ʻia me ka hemahema ʻole.

Pono e heluhelu ʻia ka Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ma hope wale nō o ka ʻauʻau ʻana, ke maʻemaʻe. I nā lā o ka menstruation, hana ʻia ka Triśandhyā i nā manawa ʻekolu e like me ka maʻamau — leo nui a i ʻole i loko o ka puʻuwai. No kēlā mau lā ʻehiku, ʻaʻole e heluhelu ʻia ka Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, ʻoiai hiki ke hoʻolohe ʻia.

I ka wā sūtak hoʻi, hiki ke hana ʻia ka Triśandhyā i nā manawa ʻekolu; akā i kēlā manawa, ʻaʻole e heluhelu ʻia a hoʻolohe ʻia ka Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, a ʻaʻole pono e hui pū me Satsaṅg. I nā hihia ʻelua, ʻaʻole e hoʻopā ʻia ka Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa a me nā mea hoʻomana ʻē aʻe.

2. He aha ka mea e hana ʻia inā ʻaʻole hiki ke hana i ka Triśandhyā piha i ka manawa kūpono?

Inā ʻaʻole hiki ke hana i ka Triśandhyā piha i ka manawa kūpono, a laila e hana i ka Triśandhyā pōkole i kēlā manawa, a e hana i ka Triśandhyā piha ma hope ke ʻae ka manawa.

Hiki ke hana ʻia ka Trikāl Sandhyā ma nā wahi a pau — ma ka wahi hana, i ka wā e hele ana ma ke alanui, i ka wā e hoʻokele ana, ma ke kula, a i ʻole i ka wā e hana ana i kekahi hana ʻē aʻe: me ka leo ʻole, i loko o ka manaʻo. ʻO ka hana ʻana i ka Triśandhyā i kona manawa kūpono, ma nā wahi a pau, he mea pono loa ia.

Inā ʻaʻole hiki ke hana i ka Trikāl Sandhyā i ka manawa kūpono, e lawe i ka inoa o ka Haku Mādhava a e mihi, a e pule i ka Haku i hiki ke hana ʻia mai kēia manawa aku i ka manawa kūpono; no ka mea, ua ʻōlelo nā palapala hemolele e pono e hana ʻia ka Trikāl Sandhyā i kona manawa kūpono.

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate।
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Pehea e hana ai nā haumāna i ka Triśandhyā me kā lākou hana maʻamau i kēlā me kēia lā?

Pono nā haumāna e hana i ka sandhyā kakahiaka (Brahma Muhūrta) a me ke ahiahi i ka manawa kūpono, e like me ke kānāwai. Ma muli o nā koi o ke kula a i ʻole ke aʻo ʻana, hiki iā lākou ke hoʻopau i ka sandhyā awakea i loko o ka puʻuwai, me ka noʻonoʻo ʻana i ka Haku i loko o ka manaʻo.

4. Pehea e hana ʻia ai ka Triśandhyā ma ka wahi hana?

ʻAʻohe kānāwai o kahi no Triśandhyā. Hiki iā ʻoe ke hana ma nā wahi a pau āu e noho nei — ma kou pākaukau, i loko o ka hale hana, i loko o ka hale kūʻai, i loko o ke kula, a i ʻole ma ke alanui.

Inā ʻaʻole hiki ke ʻōlelo leo nui, e hana i loko o ka puʻuwai; ua lawa ka puʻuwai. Inā hiki, e wehe i kou mau kāmaʻa a e noho no kekahi manawa; a inā ʻaʻole hiki kēlā, mai haʻalele i ka Triśandhyā no ia kumu.

Inā ʻaʻole ʻoe e hoʻomanaʻo a hiki, hiki iā ʻoe ke hoʻolohe i ka leo Triśandhyā. ʻO ka mea nui ʻaʻole e hala.

5. Inā ʻaʻohe manawa no ka hana hale, pehea e hahai ʻia ai ka hana Triśandhyā?

E hana i ka Triśandhyā i ka wā e hana ana ʻoe — no kēia ʻaʻole pono e kali i ka manawa kaʻawale; ʻoiai e paʻahana ana nā lima, hiki ke hana i loko o ka puʻuwai.

No ka Bhāgavata, e lawe i ka manawa liʻiliʻi āu e loaʻa ai — inā hiki ke hana i kahi mokuna piha a laila he mokuna, a inā ʻaʻole, he ʻaoʻao hoʻokahi, a i ʻole he śloka hoʻokahi. E hoʻomanaʻo ʻia kēia, ʻaʻole e hala hoʻokahi lā me ka heluhelu ʻole.

6. Pehea e hahai ai nā wahine i ka hana Triśandhyā i ka wā o ka menstruation?

I ka wā o ka menstruation, ʻaʻole e heluhelu ʻia ka Bhāgavata no nā lā ʻehiku piha. Hiki ke hoʻolohe ʻia.

E hana i ka Triśandhyā i nā manawa ʻekolu. Inā ʻaʻole ʻoe e hoʻomanaʻo, e hoʻolohe.

(Mai hoʻopā i ka Srīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa (Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa), a i ʻole nā mea hoʻomana ʻē aʻe.)

7. Inā hahai hoʻokahi kanaka i loko o ka ʻohana i ka hana Triśandhyā, e loaʻa anei i nā mea a pau kona hua?

Pono kēlā me kēia kanaka e hahai iā ia iho. Inā ʻaʻole hiki i kekahi o ka ʻohana ke heluhelu, e hoʻolohe lākou i ka Triśandhyā a me ka Bhāgavata. A no ka poʻe hiki ʻole ke ʻōlelo a lohe, e hoʻoikaika, i ka hiki, e kākau i ka inoa ‘Mādhava’ pinepine.

8. Inā ʻai mākou i ka ʻiʻo a inu i ka wai ʻona, hiki anei iā mākou ke hoʻomaka i ka Triśandhyā?

ʻAʻole, ma mua o ka hoʻomaka ʻana i ka Triśandhyā pono e ʻae i kahi ʻano ola meaʻai piha, ʻaʻole e ʻai i ka hua manu. Pono e hoʻopau ʻia nā ʻano ʻona a pau (e like me ka wai ʻona, bhāng (bhāṅg), nā paka, nā huahana paka, pān masālā, guṭkā, a pēlā aku).

9. Pono anei mākou e haʻalele i ka ʻakaʻakai a me ke kālika e hoʻomaka ai i ka Triśandhyā?

ʻAʻole, hiki iā ʻoe ke hana i ka Triśandhyā ʻoiai inā ʻoe e ʻai i ka ʻakaʻakai a me ke kālika. Pono e pale ʻia nā ʻano ʻiʻo a pau (me ka hua manu).

10. Hiki anei i nā wahine ke oli i ka Gāyatrī Mantra a me Triśandhyā?

ʻAe, pono nā wahine e oli i ka Gāyatrī Mantra ʻekolu manawa i ka wā Triśandhyā.

11. Hiki anei iā mākou ke oli i ka Gāyatrī Mantra me ka Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu) ʻole?

ʻAe, hiki iā ʻoe ke oli i ka Gāyatrī Mantra ʻoiai inā ʻaʻole ʻoe e ʻaʻahu i ke kaula hemolele janeu. Hiki iā ʻoe ke heluhelu i nā mantra a pau o Triśandhyā me ka dīkṣit ʻole i kahi guru hoʻi.

12. Hiki anei iā mākou ke oli i ka Gāyatrī Mantra me ka Śāpa Vimocana (kala ʻana i ka hōʻino) ʻole?

ʻAe, pono ʻoe e oli i ka Gāyatrī Mantra ʻekolu manawa i ka wā e hana ana i ka Triśandhyā, me ka hana ʻole i kekahi kala ʻana i ka hōʻino.

13. Inā he kanaka heluhelu ʻole, kīnā ʻole, pēpē a i ʻole he ʻelemakule, hiki ʻole ke heluhelu i ka Triśandhyā, he aha ka mea e hana ai?

Inā hiki ʻole i kekahi ke heluhelu i ka kikokikona Triśandhyā, hiki iā lākou ke hoʻolohe i kāna leo ʻekolu manawa i ka lā i ka wā Triśandhyā. Ua hoʻokuʻu ʻia nā pēpē inā hana ko lākou mau makuahine i ka Triśandhyā (e pōmaikaʻi lākou). Eia naʻe, pono lākou e hana i ka Triśandhyā i ko lākou ulu ʻana.
