Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"Kuphucuko lwabantu, Triśandhyā injengeSanjivani (Sañjīvanī) (umuthi wokuphila)."

Yenza Triśandhyā ngazo zonke izikhathi ezintathu ze-sandhyā nsuku zonke, funda isahluko esisodwa Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa nsuku zonke, futhi uhlabelele igama likaMadhava (Mādhava) njalo engqondweni.

Bamba lezi zimfundiso ezi-5 ezilandelayo kanye Trikāl Sandhyā

1. Funda ukulalela.

2. Funda ukulinda.

3. Funda ukuthanda nokubonisa ububele kuzo zonke izidalwa eziphilayo.

4. Gcina ukuzithiba phezu kwezinzwa.

5. Lahla umuzwa wokuthi “Mina nokwami, wena nokwakho” (umqondo wobugovu).

Imiyalo Ebalulekile

Ukuze kukhululwe enkingeni enkulu ezayo, ngokwe-‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā’, yeka izenzo ezinesono futhi uvumele bonke abantu bamukele i-‘Triśandhyā’ ukuze kuvikelwe impilo. Ngesikhathi sokuguquka kwesikhathi, ukuze kungene Satyayuga, lesi simiso esiyimfihlo Mṛta Sañjīvanī eyodwa kuphela.
Ngesikhathi sokusungulwa Dharma, ngokwemiyalo yeNkosi Kalki, kufanele silandele lezi zinto ezimpilweni zethu:

1. Bonisa ububele kuzo zonke izidalwa eziphilayo. Yeka ukudla inyama futhi wamukele impilo ye-sāttvic. Ungalimazi noma yisiphi isidalwa esiphilayo noma umuntu nganoma iyiphi indlela.

2. Kulesi sikhathi, hlabelela igama likaMadhava (Mādhava) (iNkosi). Uma ufisa, ungahlabelela phezulu noma ukhumbule igama Lakhe ngokuthula engqondweni.

3. Wonke umuntu kufanele abambelele kulezi zimfanelo ezinhlanu: Iqiniso, Uthando, Ububele, Ukuthethelela kanye Nokuthula.

4. Wonke umuntu kufanele abambe iqhaza Satsaṅg njalo. Satsaṅg isho ukubuthana ndawonye ukuhlabelela izimfanelo zikaNkulunkulu, ukuchaza inkazimulo Yakhe, nokubamba ingxoxo engokomoya.

5. Yenza Triśandhyā nsuku zonke — khumbula futhi udumise iNkosi ekuseni, emini nasebusuku.

6. Hlabelela Kalki Mahāmantra enikezwe yiNkosi Kalki nsuku zonke.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Wonke umuntu kufanele ahambe endleleni Dharma.

8. Ukuphakama ngaphezu kobandlululo lobuhlanga nenkolo, yenza Satsaṅg nawo wonke umuntu.

9. Lahla umuzwa wokuthi “Mina” nokwami.” Ukuziqhenya nokunamathela okunjalo kuqhelelanisa umuntu nemvelo yakhe yangempela engokomoya.

10. Funda Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ekhaya nsuku zonke. Njengoba isikhathi sivuma, funda i-bhajan, i-kīrtan, amaPurana (Purāṇas), imibhalo, Bhaviṣya Mālikā neminye imibhalo engokomoya.

11. Biza wonke owesifazane ngokuthi “Mama”/“Mā” nawo wonke owesilisa ngokuthi “Mfowethu.”

12. Ngaphambi kokudla, qala unikele ukudla eNkosini.

13. Uma ufisa ukwazi okwengeziwe ngeNkosi Kalki nalo msebenzi omkhulu, xhumana nenombolo yeselula enikezwe ekugcineni kwale ncwajana.


Imibuzo Evame Kakhulu

1. Imibuzo ejwayelekile mayelana nomkhuba Triśandhyā

Triśandhyā ingenziwa ngaphandle kokugeza. Beka isandla sakho ebunzini lakho bese ukhumbula uMama Gaṅgā, uthi “Śrī Gaṅge” izikhathi ezintathu. Uma kungenzeka, geza izinyawo zakho ngaphambi kwesikhathi bese ufafaza amanzi amancane kuwe.

Triśandhyā ingenziwa noma kuphi — emgwaqweni, esitimeleni, emotweni, endaweni yakho yomsebenzi, noma ngokuthula enhliziyweni. Nganoma iyiphi indlela, kunoma iyiphi indawo, kufanele yenziwe ngaphandle kokwehluleka.

Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa kufanele ifundwe kuphela ngemuva kokugeza, uma ihlanzekile. Ngezinsuku zokuya esikhathini Triśandhyā yenziwa ngazo zonke izikhathi ezintathu njengokujwayelekile — phezulu noma enhliziyweni. Kulezo zinsuku eziyisikhombisa Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa akufanele ifundwe, nakuba ingalalelwa.

Ngesikhathi se-sūtak futhi, Triśandhyā ingenziwa ngazo zonke izikhathi ezintathu; kodwa ngaleso sikhathi Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa akufanele ifundwe noma ilalelwe, futhi Satsaṅg akufanele ihlanganyelwe. Kuzo zombili izimo Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa nezinye izinto zokukhulekela akufanele zithintwe.

2. Yini okufanele yenziwe uma Triśandhyā ephelele ingenakwenzeka ngesikhathi esifanele?

Uma Triśandhyā ephelele ingenakwenzeka ngesikhathi esifanele, yenza iTriśandhyā emfushane ngaleso sikhathi, bese wenza iTriśandhyā ephelele kamuva uma isikhathi sivuma.

Trikāl Sandhyā ingenziwa noma kuphi — endaweni yomsebenzi, noma uhamba emgwaqweni, noma ushayela, esikoleni, noma ngisho noma wenza omunye umsebenzi: ngokuthula, engqondweni. Ukwenza Triśandhyā ngesikhathi sayo esifanele, kunoma iyiphi indawo, kubaluleke kakhulu.

Uma Trikāl Sandhyā ingenakwenziwa ngesikhathi esifanele, thatha igama leNkosi Mādhava bese uphenduka, futhi uthandaze eNkosini ukuthi kusukela manje iyenziwe ngesikhathi esifanele; ngoba imibhalo ithi Trikāl Sandhyā kufanele yenziwe ngesikhathi sayo esifanele.

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate।
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Abafundi kufanele bayenze kanjani Triśandhyā kanye nesimiso sabo sansuku zonke?

Abafundi kufanele benze i-sandhyā yasekuseni (Brahma Muhurta (Brahma Muhūrta)) neyakusihlwa ngesikhathi esifanele, ngokomthetho. Ngenxa yezidingo zesikole noma zokufunda, bangase baqedele i-sandhyā yasemini enhiziyweni, bemeditata ngeNkosi engqondweni.

4. Kufanele yenziwe kanjani Triśandhyā endaweni yomsebenzi?

Akukho mthetho wendawo Triśandhyā. Ungayenza noma ngabe ukuphi — edeskini lakho, efemini, esitolo, ensimini, noma emgwaqweni.

Uma kungenakwenzeka ukukusho phezulu, kwenze enhiziyweni; inhliziyo yindawo eyanele. Uma kungenzeka, khumula izicathulo zakho bese uhlala isikhashana; futhi uma ngisho nalokho kungenakwenzeka, ungayishiyi Triśandhyā ngenxa yaleso sizathu.

Uma ungayikhumbuli futhi kungenzeka, ungase ulalele umsindo Triśandhyā. Okubalulekile ukuthi ingaphuthelwa.

5. Uma umsebenzi wasekhaya ungasishiyi isikhathi, kufanele ulandelwe kanjani umkhuba Triśandhyā?

Yenza Triśandhyā noma usebenza — ngalokhu akukho sikhathi samahhala okufanele silindelwe; ngisho noma izandla zimatasatasa kungenziwa enhiziyweni.

Bhāgavata, khipha noma yisiphi isikhathi esincane ongathola — uma isahluko esiphelele kungenzeka bese kuba isahluko, futhi uma kungenjalo, ikhasi elilodwa, noma ngisho ne-śloka eyodwa. Lokhu kufanele kugcinwe engqondweni, ukuthi akukho ngisho nosuku olulodwa oludlulayo ngaphandle kokufunda.

6. Abesifazane kufanele bawulandele kanjani umkhuba Triśandhyā ngesikhathi sokuya esikhathini?

Ngesikhathi sokuya esikhathini, Bhāgavata akufanele ifundwe izinsuku eziyisikhombisa eziphelele. Ingalalelwa.

Yenza Triśandhyā ngazo zonke izikhathi ezintathu. Uma ungayikhumbuli, yilalele.

(Ungathinti iShrimad Bhagavata Mahapurana (Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa), noma ezinye izinto zokukhulekela.)

7. Uma umuntu oyedwa emndenini elandela umkhuba Triśandhyā, ingabe bonke bazothola isithelo sawo?

Kubalulekile ukuthi wonke umuntu akulandele yena uqobo. Uma umuntu emndenini engakwazi ukufunda, makalalele Triśandhyā Bhāgavata. Futhi kulabo abangakwazi ukukhuluma noma ukuzwa, yenza umzamo, ngangokunokwenzeka, wokuba babhale igama elithi ‘Mādhava’ kaningi.

8. Uma sidla inyama futhi siphuza utshwala, singaqala Triśandhyā?

Cha, ngaphambi kokuqala Triśandhyā umuntu kufanele amukele indlela yokuphila yemifino ngokuphelele, akufanele ngisho adle iqanda. Noma yiluphi uhlobo lokudakwa (njengotshwala, i-bhang (bhāṅg), ugwayi, imikhiqizo kagwayi, i-pān masālā, i-guṭkā, njll.) kufanele futhi kumiswe ngokuqinile.

9. Ingabe sidinga ukuyeka u-anyanisi nogalikhi ukuze siqale Triśandhyā?

Cha, ungenza Triśandhyā ngisho noma udla u-anyanisi nogalikhi. Kuphela zonke izinhlobo zenyama akufanele zidliwe (kuhlanganise neqanda).

10. Ingabe abesifazane bangahlabelela Gāyatrī Mantra Triśandhyā?

Yebo, abesifazane kufanele bahlabelele Gāyatrī Mantra kathathu ngesikhathi Triśandhyā.

11. Singahlabelela Gāyatrī Mantra ngaphandle kwe-Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu)?

Yebo, ungahlabelela Gāyatrī Mantra ngisho noma ungagqoki intambo engcwele ye-janeu. Ungaphinda wonke ama-mantra Triśandhyā ngaphandle kokuba u-dīkṣit kum-guru futhi.

12. Singahlabelela Gāyatrī Mantra ngaphandle Śāpa Vimocana (ukukhululwa esiqalekisweni)?

Yebo, kufanele uhlabelele Gāyatrī Mantra kathathu ngesikhathi wenza Triśandhyā, ngaphandle kokwenza noma yikuphi ukukhululwa esiqalekisweni.

13. Uma umuntu engafundile, okhubazekile, usana noma umuntu osekhulile, ongenakho ukufunda Triśandhyā, kufanele enzeni?

Uma umuntu engenakho ukufunda umbhalo Triśandhyā, angakwazi ukulalela umsindo wawo kathathu ngosuku ngesikhathi Triśandhyā. Izinsana zikhululiwe uma omama bazo benza Triśandhyā (zizozuza). Nokho, kufanele zizijwayeze Triśandhyā njengoba zikhula.
