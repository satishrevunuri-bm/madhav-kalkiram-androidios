Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"Mihring khawvel tan, Triśandhyā hi Sañjīvanī ang a ni."

Ni tin sandhyā hun thumah Triśandhyā ti rawh, ni tin Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa bung khat chhiar rawh, leh rilruin Mādhava hming chu chawl lova lam rawh.

Trikāl Sandhyā nen zirtirna 5 te hi vuan rawh

1. Thu awih zir rawh.

2. Nghah zir rawh.

3. Thil nung zawng zawng hmangaih leh khawngaih zir rawh.

4. Mahni taksa hriatna chungah inthununna nei rawh.

5. “Kei leh ka ta, nang leh i ta” tih ngaihtuahna (mahni inngaihlu tlatna) chu kalsan rawh.

Tlai ni tla hma minute sawmthum leh ni tla hnu minute sawmthum inkarah tih theih a ni

Thupui Pawimawh Te

Lo thleng tur harsatna lian tak laka chhan chhuah nan, ‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā’ sawi danin, sualna thil tihte bansan la, mi zawng zawngin nun humhimna atan ‘Triśandhyā’ hi vuan rawh se. Hun inher danglamnaah, Satyayuga-a luh theih nan, he thuruk pawimawh hi Mṛta Sañjīvanī pakhat chauh a ni.
Dharma din hunah, Bhagavān Kalki thupêk angin, kan nunah heng thil te hi kan zâwm tur a ni:

1. Thil nung zawng zawngte khawngaih rawh. Sa ei bansan la, sāttvic nun vuan rawh. Thil nung engmah leh mihring tumah engtin mah ti na suh.

2. He hunah hian, Mādhava (Bhagavān) hming chu lam rawh. I duh chuan, ring takin i lam thei a, a nih loh chuan rilruin ngawi renga a hming chu hre reng rawh.

3. Mi tinin heng nungchang tha panga te hi vuan rawh se: Dikna, Hmangaihna, Khawngaihna, Ngaihdamna leh Remna.

4. Mi tinin Satsaṅg-ah regular takin tel rawh se. Satsaṅg tih awmzia chu Pathian mize tha te sa tur, A ropuina sawi tur, leh thlarau lam thil sawi ho turin inkhawm ho tihna a ni.

5. Ni tin Triśandhyā ti rawh — zing, chhun leh tlaiin Bhagavān chu hre reng la, fak rawh.

6. Ni tin Bhagavān Kalki pêk Kalki Mahāmantra chu lam rawh.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Mi tinin Dharma kawngah kal rawh se.

8. Hnam leh sakhaw thliar hranna chungah chhoin, mi zawng zawng nen Satsaṅg nei rawh.

9. “Kei” leh “ka ta” tih ngaihtuahna chu kalsan rawh. Chutiang chapona leh innghahna chuan mihring chu an thlarau lam nihna dik tak atangin a hruai hrang thin.

10. Ni tin inah Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa chhiar rawh. Hun i neih ang zelin, bhajan, kīrtan, Purāṇas, Pathian Lehkha Thiamna, Bhaviṣya Mālikā leh thlarau lam lehkhabu dangte zir rawh.

11. Hmeichhe tin chu “Nu”/“Mā” tiin ko la, mipa tin chu “Unaupa” tiin ko rawh.

12. Ei hmain, chaw chu Bhagavān hnenah hmasa berin hlan rawh.

13. Bhagavān Kalki leh he hna ropui tak chungchang hriat belh i duh chuan, he lehkhabu tawp lama mobile number pêk ah hian biak theih a ni.


Zawhna Tam Berte

1. Triśandhyā tih dan chungchang zawhna hrang hrangte

Triśandhyā hi inbual lovin tih theih a ni. I kut chu i chalah dah la, Nu Gaṅgā hre rengin, “Śrī Gaṅge” vawi thum sawi rawh. Theih a nih chuan, i ke chu sil hmasa la, tui tlem i taksaah theh rawh.

Triśandhyā hi khawi hmunah pawh tih theih a ni — kawngah, rel-ah, motor-ah, i hna hmunah, a nih loh chuan rilruin ngawi renga. Engtin emaw, khawi hmunah emaw, tih ngei ngei tur a ni.

Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa hi inbual hnuah chauh, thianghlim tawh hnuah chhiar tur a ni. Hmeichhe thi neih lai ni-ah Triśandhyā hi hun thumah pangngai takin tih tur a ni — ring takin emaw rilruin emaw. Ni sarih chhung chu Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa hi chhiar loh tur a ni, mahse ngaihthlak theih a ni.

sūtak lai pawhin, Triśandhyā hi hun thumah tih theih a ni; mahse chu hunah chuan Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa hi chhiar emaw ngaihthlak emaw loh tur a ni, leh Satsaṅg-ah tel loh tur a ni. Thil pahnihah hian Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa leh Pathian biakna thil dangte chu khawih loh tur a ni.

2. Triśandhyā pumpui hun takah tih theih loh chuan engtin nge tih tur?

Triśandhyā pumpui hun takah tih theih loh chuan, chu hunah chuan Triśandhyā tawi ti la, hun i neih hunah Triśandhyā pumpui chu hnuah ti rawh.

Trikāl Sandhyā hi khawi hmunah pawh tih theih a ni — hna hmunah, kawngah kal chungin, motor khalh chungin, sikulah, a nih loh chuan hna dang engpawh tih chungin: ngawi renga, rilruin. Triśandhyā chu a hun takah, khawi hmunah pawh ni se, tih ngei ngei tur a ni.

Trikāl Sandhyā chu a hun takah tih theih loh chuan, Bhagavān Mādhava hming lam la, sim rawh, leh tun atanga a hun takah tih theih nan Bhagavān hnenah tawngtai rawh; Pathian Lehkha Thiamna chuan Trikāl Sandhyā chu a hun takah tih tur a ni tih a puang si a.

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate।
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Zirlaiten an ni tin tih tur nen Triśandhyā engtin nge an tih ang?

Zirlaiten zing (Brahma Muhūrta) leh tlai sandhyā chu a hun takah, dan ang zelin ti tur a ni. Sikul emaw zirna avanga hun neih loh vangin, chhun sandhyā chu rilruin, Bhagavān chu ngaihtuahin an ti thei ang.

4. Hna hmunah Triśandhyā engtin nge tih tur?

Triśandhyā atan hmun dan a awm lo. Khawi hmunah pawh i awmnaah i ti thei — i dawhkanah, factory-ah, dawrah, loah, a nih loh chuan kawngah.

Ring taka sawi theih loh chuan, rilruin ti rawh; rilru chu hmun tawk a ni. Theih a nih chuan, i pheikhawk phelh la, reilote thu rawh; chu pawh theih loh chuan, chu vang chuan Triśandhyā chu kalsan suh.

I hriat reng loh a, theih a nih chuan, Triśandhyā audio chu i ngaithla thei. Thil pawimawh ber chu a hmaih loh hi a ni.

5. In chhung hna vangin hun awl a awm loh chuan, Triśandhyā tih dan engtin nge zawm tur?

I hna thawh laiin Triśandhyā ti rawh — hei atan hian hun awl nghah a ngai lo; kut a buai lai pawhin rilruin tih theih a ni.

Bhāgavata atan, hun tlemte i hmuh theih ang ang chu hmang rawh — bung khat pumpui theih a nih chuan bung khat, a nih loh chuan phek khat, a nih loh pawhin śloka pakhat pawh. Hei hi hriat reng tur a ni, ni khat pawh chhiar lovin a liam tur a ni lo.

6. Hmeichhiaten thi neih laiin Triśandhyā tih dan engtin nge an zawm ang?

Thi neih laiin, Bhāgavata hi ni sarih chhung zawng chhiar loh tur a ni. Ngaihthlak theih a ni.

Triśandhyā chu hun thumah ti rawh. I hriat reng loh chuan, ngaithla rawh.

(Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, emaw Pathian biakna thil dangte chu khawih loh tur a ni.)

7. Chhungkuaa mi pakhatin Triśandhyā tih dan a zawm chuan, an zaa chuan a rah an dawng vek ang em?

Mi tinin mahni ngeiin zawm a ngai. Chhungkuaa mi pakhatin chhiar thei lo a nih chuan, Triśandhyā leh Bhāgavata chu ngaithla rawh se. Leh tawng thei lo emaw hriat thei lo tan chuan, theih ang tawkin, ‘Mādhava’ hming chu an ziak nawn fo theih nan hma la rawh.

8. Sa leh zu kan in chuan, Triśandhyā kan tan thei ang em?

Aih, Triśandhyā tan hmain, chawhmeh chauh ei tur a ni, artui pawh ei loh tur a ni. Ruihtheih thil eng chi pawh (zu, bhāṅg, cigarette, vaihlo thil siam, pān masālā, guṭkā, etc.) chu bansan vek tur a ni.

9. Triśandhyā tan turin Purun leh Balhnam ei loh a ngai em?

Aih, Purun leh Balhnam i ei chung pawhin Triśandhyā i ti thei. Sa chi hrang hrang zawng zawng chauh chu pumpelh tur a ni (artui telin).

10. Hmeichhiaten Gāyatrī Mantra leh Triśandhyā an lam thei em?

Aw, hmeichhiaten Triśandhyā laiin Gāyatrī Mantra vawi thum an lam tur a ni.

11. Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu) tel lovin Gāyatrī Mantra kan lam thei em?

Aw, janeu thianghlim i ha lo chung pawhin Gāyatrī Mantra i lam thei. Triśandhyā mantras zawng zawng chu guru hnenah dīkṣit ni lovin pawh i lam thei.

12. Śāpa Vimocana (anchhia lak kianna) tel lovin Gāyatrī Mantra kan lam thei em?

Aw, Triśandhyā tih laiin Gāyatrī Mantra chu vawi thum i lam tur a ni, anchhia lak kianna engmah ti lovin.

13. Lehkha thiam lo, taksa hrisel lo, nausen emaw upa tawh, Triśandhyā chhiar thei lo an nih chuan, engtin nge an tih ang?

Triśandhyā text chhiar thei lo an awm chuan, Triśandhyā hunah ni khatah vawi thum a audio chu an ngaithla thei. Nausente chu an nuin Triśandhyā an tih chuan an chhuah zalen a ni (an hlawkpui ang). Amaherawhchu, an lo puitlin hunah Triśandhyā hi an ti ngei tur a ni.
