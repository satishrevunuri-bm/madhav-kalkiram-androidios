Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ

॥ Jaya Śrī Mādhava ॥

"mānuwa jāti laṭhiṅ, Triśandhyā do Sañjīvanī lekaṅa."

Dinahiṅ pe sandhyā waqt re Triśandhyā do, dinahiṅ Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa reyaḥ miṭṭaḥ adhyāy paṛhāw do, ar Mādhava ñañum do mon bhitar re nirantar jap do.

Trikāl Sandhyā saṅge ināḥ 5ṭi śikṣā do dharao.

1. Mānao do cet' do.

2. Roka do cet' do.

3. Saṅge jīw jantu ko do dulāṛ do ar dayā do cet' do.

4. Indriya ko upar re saṅyam do doho.

5. “Iṅ ar iṅaḥ, āṅ ar āṅaḥ” (ahaṅkār reyaḥ bhāwnā) do bāgi do.

Triśandhyā reyaḥ pe waqt.

Setaḥ. Brahma Muhūrta re, hasur lekaṅaḥ.
Tikin. between 11:30 and 12:30
Ayup. may be done between thirty minutes before and thirty minutes after sunset

Triśandhyā reyaḥ biṣay.

Jaya Śrī Mādhava


Marāṅ nirddeś.

Hiju' kan marāṅ saṅkaṭ khon bachāw laṭhiṅ, ‘Bhaviṣya Mālikā’ lekaṅaḥ, pāp kāmī do bāgi do ar saṅge hor' do jīwan bachāw laṭhiṅ ‘Triśandhyā’ do apnāw do. Yug badal waqt re, Satyayuga re dhāṅgāw laṭhiṅ, noa gupta siddhānt do miṭṭaḥ ar miṭṭaḥ Mṛta Sañjīvanī kana.
Dharma sthāpan waqt re, Bhagavān Kalki reyaḥ nirddeś lekaṅaḥ, ābo do ābo'aḥ jīwan re noa katha ko do mānao do:

1. Saṅge jīw jantu ko upar re dayā do. Hāku-jil' jom do bāgi do ar miṭṭaḥ sāttvic jīwan do apnāw do. Jahaṅ jīw jantu do bāṅkhan jahaṅ mānuwa do jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ do alom kasta do.

2. Noa yug re, Mādhava (Bhagavān) reyaḥ ñañum do jap do. Jo di āṅ do mon do, āṅ do roṛ do bāṅkhan mon bhitar re uñum do.

3. Saṅge hor' do noa peṭṭaḥ guṇ do dharao do: Satya, Dulāṛ, Dayā, Kṣamā ar Śānti.

4. Saṅge hor' do niyamit' rupe Satsaṅg re bhāg do. Satsaṅg do Isvar reyaḥ guṇ do serenj do, Uni'aḥ mahimā do bakhān do, ar ādhyātmik' ālōcanā do.

5. Dinahiṅ Triśandhyā do — setaḥ, tikin ar ayup re Bhagavān do uñum do ar prasaṅsā do.

6. Dinahiṅ Bhagavān Kalki do em' akana Kalki Mahāmantra do jap do.

Kalki Mahāmantra Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare ।
Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare, Anaṃta Mādhava Hare ॥

7. Saṅge hor' do Dharma reyaḥ hor' re do calāw do.

8. Jāti ar dharmik' bhedbhāw khon upar do, saṅge hor' saṅge Satsaṅg do.

9. “Iṅ” ar “iṅaḥ” reyaḥ bhāwnā do bāgi do. Noa lekaṅaḥ garab ar moh do miṭṭaḥ hor' do uni'aḥ satya ādhyātmik' prakṛti khon do saro do.

10. Dinahiṅ oṛak' re Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa do paṛhāw do. Waṭk' jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ do em' do, bhajan, kīrtan, Purāṇas, śāstra, Bhaviṣya Mālikā ar itar ādhyātmik' pustak ko do adhyayan do.

11. Saṅge ayo do “Ayo”/“Mā” ar saṅge hopon do “Bhāy” lekaṅaḥ do sambodhan do.

12. Jom lekaṅaḥ do, pratham do Bhagavān do bhog do.

13. Jo di āṅ do Bhagavān Kalki ar noa marāṅ kāmī bāre re besī do baḍāy do, noa pustak reyaḥ mud re em' akana mobāil' saṅkhyā do saṅpark do.


Besī sāmānya praśna.

1. Triśandhyā abhyās bāre re sāmānya praśna.

Triśandhyā do udup' bāṅkhan do hoy' do. Āṅaḥ hoṛmo upar re ti' do doho do ar Gaṅgā ayo do uñum do, “Śrī Gaṅge” pe bār roṛ do. Jo di hoy' do, lekaṅaḥ do āṅaḥ kāṭā do oḍok' do ar āṅaḥ upar re miṭṭaḥ kamī do dāk' do chitao do.

Triśandhyā do jahaṅ re do hoy' do — hor' re, ṭren re, gāṛī re, kāmī ṭhāw re, bāṅkhan mon bhitar re chup'. Miṭṭaḥ rūp re bāṅkhan itar rūp re, jahaṅ ṭhāw re do, noa do bāṅkhan do hoy' do.

Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa do udup' lekaṅaḥ do paṛhāw do, jakhon do sapha. Māhāwārī din ko re Triśandhyā do saṅge pe waqt re sāmānya lekaṅaḥ do hoy' do — roṛ do bāṅkhan mon bhitar re. Noa gel' din laṭhiṅ Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa do paṛhāw do bāṅkhan do, jo di āñjom do hoy' do.

sūtak waqt re do, Triśandhyā do saṅge pe waqt re do hoy' do; menkhan noa waqt re Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa do paṛhāw do bāṅkhan do bāṅkhan āñjom do bāṅkhan do, ar Satsaṅg do bāṅkhan do. Duin'o khetro re Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa ar itar pūjā sāmāgrī do alok' do chūw do.

2. Jo di pūrṇa Triśandhyā do ṭhik waqt re bāṅkhan do, ināḥ do cet' do?

Jo di pūrṇa Triśandhyā do ṭhik waqt re bāṅkhan do, ināḥ waqt re do kamī Triśandhyā do, ar waqt hoy' lekaṅaḥ do pūrṇa Triśandhyā do parom do.

Trikāl Sandhyā do jahaṅ re do hoy' do — kāmī ṭhāw re, hor' re calāw waqt re, gāṛī calāw waqt re, iskul re, bāṅkhan jahaṅ itar kāmī do waqt re do: mon bhitar re chup'. Triśandhyā do uni'aḥ ṭhik waqt re, jahaṅ ṭhāw re do, atyanta āwaśyak' kana.

Jo di Trikāl Sandhyā do ṭhik waqt re bāṅkhan do, Bhagavān Mādhava reyaḥ ñañum do ar paśtāw do, ar Bhagavān do prārthanā do je noa khon do ṭhik waqt re do hoy' do; kārṇa śāstra ko do roṛ do je Trikāl Sandhyā do uni'aḥ ṭhik waqt re do hoy' do.

Trikāl Sandhyā reyaḥ bidhi jakhon waqt bāṅkhan do.

Daśāvatāra Stotram (Aṃtima Śloka)

Vedān uddharate jagannivahate bhūgolam udbibhrate
daityaṃ dārayate baliṃ chalayate kṣatrakṣayaṃ kurvate।
paulastyaṃ jayate halaṃ kalayate kāruṇyam ātanvate
mlecchān mūrchayate daśākṛtikṛte Kṛṣṇāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ॥

3. Chātra ko do uni'aḥ dinahiṅ kāmī saṅge Triśandhyā do jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ do?

Chātra ko do setaḥ (Brahma Muhūrta) ar ayup sandhyā do ṭhik waqt re, niyam lekaṅaḥ do. Iskul bāṅkhan adhyayan reyaḥ māṅg kārṇa, uni ko do tikin sandhyā do mon bhitar re, Bhagavān do mon re dhyān do.

4. Kāmī ṭhāw re Triśandhyā do jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ do?

Triśandhyā laṭhiṅ ṭhāw reyaḥ niyam bāṅkhan do. Āṅ do jahaṅ re menak' do — āṅaḥ ḍesk re, kārkhānā re, dukān re, khet' re, bāṅkhan hor' re.

Jo di roṛ do bāṅkhan do, mon bhitar re do; mon do ṭhāw do besī kana. Jo di hoy' do, āṅaḥ jūtā do oḍok' do ar miṭṭaḥ kṣaṇ laṭhiṅ do dub' do; ar jo di ināḥ do bāṅkhan do, ināḥ kārṇa Triśandhyā do alom bāgi do.

Jo di āṅ do bāṅkhan do ar jo di hoy' do, āṅ do Triśandhyā audio do āñjom do. Mukhy katha do noa do je noa do bāṅkhan do.

5. Jo di oṛak' kāmī do waqt bāṅkhan do, Triśandhyā abhyās do jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ do mānao do?

Triśandhyā do āṅ kāmī waqt re — noa laṭhiṅ do khāli waqt do bāṅkhan do; ti' do byast waqt re do mon bhitar re do hoy' do.

Bhāgavata laṭhiṅ, jahaṅ kamī waqt do āṅ do ñam do — jo di pūrṇa adhyāy do hoy' do ināḥ adhyāy, ar jo di bāṅkhan do, miṭṭaḥ pṛṣṭhā, bāṅkhan miṭṭaḥ śloka. Noa do mon re doho do, je miṭṭaḥ din do bāṅkhan do paṛhāw bāṅkhan do.

6. Māhāwārī waqt re ayo ko do Triśandhyā abhyās do jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ do mānao do?

Māhāwārī waqt re, Bhāgavata do pūrṇa gel' din laṭhiṅ do paṛhāw do bāṅkhan do. Noa do āñjom do hoy' do.

Triśandhyā do saṅge pe waqt re do. Jo di āṅ do bāṅkhan do, ināḥ do āñjom do.

(Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, bāṅkhan itar pūjā sāmāgrī do alok' do chūw do.)

7. Jo di miṭṭaḥ pariwār re miṭṭaḥ hor' do Triśandhyā abhyās do mānao do, saṅge ko do uni'aḥ phal do ñam do?

Noa do āwaśyak' kana je saṅge hor' do uni ko do mānao do. Jo di pariwār re jahaṅ hor' do paṛhāw do bāṅkhan do, uni ko do Triśandhyā ar Bhāgavata do āñjom do. Ar uni ko laṭhiṅ jo roṛ do bāṅkhan āñjom do bāṅkhan do, prayās do, jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ do hoy' do, uni ko do ‘Mādhava’ ñañum do ol' do bār bār.

8. Jo di ābo do nonveg jom do ar dāru do nu' do, ābo do Triśandhyā do ārambh do hoy' do?

Bāṅ, Triśandhyā ārambh lekaṅaḥ do miṭṭaḥ hor' do pūrṇa śākāhārī jīwan śailī do apnāw do, aṇḍā do bāṅkhan do. Jahaṅ lekaṅaḥ nāsā (lekaṅaḥ dāru, bhāṅg, sigareṭ, tambāku utpād, pān masālā, guṭkā, itar) do bāṅkhan do niścit' rupe.

9. Triśandhyā ārambh laṭhiṅ piyāj ar rasun khon do bāṅkhan do?

Bāṅ, āṅ do Triśandhyā do hoy' do jo di āṅ do piyāj ar rasun do jom do. Keval' saṅge lekaṅaḥ non veg do bāṅkhan do (aṇḍā saṅge).

10. Ayō ko do Gāyatrī Mantra ar Triśandhyā do jap do hoy' do?

Hẽ, ayō ko do Triśandhyā waqt re Gāyatrī Mantra do pe bār jap do.

11. Ābo do Gāyatrī Mantra do jap do hoy' do Upanayana Saṃskāra (janeu) bāṅkhan do?

Hẽ, āṅ do Gāyatrī Mantra do jap do hoy' do jo di āṅ do pavitra janeu sutam do bāṅkhan do. Āṅ do Triśandhyā reyaḥ saṅge mantra ko do pāṭh do hoy' do guru do dīkṣit bāṅkhan do.

12. Ābo do Gāyatrī Mantra do jap do hoy' do Śāpa Vimocana (śāp mukti) bāṅkhan do?

Hẽ, āṅ do Triśandhyā do waqt re Gāyatrī Mantra do pe bār jap do, jahaṅ śāp mukti do bāṅkhan do.

13. Jo di miṭṭaḥ hor' do anpaṛh, alag' rupe sakṣam, hopon bāṅkhan buṛhā-buṛhī, Triśandhyā paṛhāw do bāṅkhan do, uni ko do cet' do?

Jo di jahaṅ hor' do Triśandhyā pāṭh do bāṅkhan do, uni ko do Triśandhyā waqt re uni'aḥ audio do din re pe bār āñjom do. Hopon ko do chūṭ' do jo di uni'aḥ ayo ko do Triśandhyā do (uni ko do lābh do ñam do). Menkhan, uni ko do marāṅ hoy' lekaṅaḥ do Triśandhyā do abhyās do.
