Privacy Policy

Kalkiram: Bhavishya Malika & Trisandhya Mobile Application

Operated by Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ (kalkiram.com)

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

🛡️ Summary: We respect your privacy. The Kalkiram app is purely spiritual and educational. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, and our app does not serve any advertisements.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information in connection with the mobile application Kalkiram (including its Bhavishya Malika guidance, Trisandhya prayer schedule, multilingual PDFs, and audio features).

2. Information Collection and Use

The application is designed to function with complete user privacy:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or name to use the app.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect your personal identity, contact details, precise location, contacts, or financial information.
  • No Tracking: We do not track your activity across other applications or websites.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The application requests only minimal, necessary device permissions strictly required to provide its core spiritual features:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required exclusively to download and stream multilingual Bhavishya Malika PDFs and Trisandhya audio files in 125+ languages from official servers.
  • Network State (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to determine whether your device has an active internet connection before initiating PDF downloads or audio streams.
  • Local Storage / Preferences: Used locally on your device to store offline downloaded PDFs, bookmark your reading position, and remember your language preference. This data never leaves your device.

4. Advertising and Analytics

  • No Advertisements: The app contains zero third-party ads. We do not use AdMob or any other advertising SDK.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We do not integrate data brokers, behavioral analytics, or marketing trackers.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our application contains religious, spiritual, and educational content that is safe for individuals of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13 in accordance with COPPA, GDPR-K, and applicable global child privacy laws.

6. Data Security

All network communications between the application and our servers (for retrieving PDFs and audio) are conducted over industry-standard encrypted HTTPS (SSL/TLS) connections.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this web page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
