## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines the practices of **Viśva Sanātana Dharma Sevā Ṭrusṭ** regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through the **Kalkiram** mobile application (covering Bhavishya Malika guidance, Trisandhya prayer schedule, multilingual PDFs, and audio).

## 2. Information Collection and Use
The application respects user privacy:
- **No User Accounts:** You are not required to create an account or provide personal details to use this application.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device contacts.
- **No User Tracking:** We do not monitor or track user behavior across apps or websites.

## 3. Permissions Used
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Used exclusively to stream and download Bhavishya Malika PDFs and Trisandhya audio in 125+ languages.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Checks internet connectivity status before attempting network calls.
- `Local Storage / Preferences`: Saves user preferences (e.g. selected language, reading bookmarks) and cached PDFs locally on device for offline reading.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
- **No Ads:** The application is 100% ad-free.
- **No Analytics Trackers:** No third-party data analytics or tracking SDKs are incorporated.

## 5. Children's Privacy
The app contains spiritual and educational content suitable for all audiences. We do not collect any personal information from children or any other users.
